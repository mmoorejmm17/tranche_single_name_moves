## 2. Verified terms

**ABL facility** (10-K FY2025; Q2 2026 10-Q)
- Borrower Xerox Corp; Holdings and certain subsidiaries guarantee. Citibank, N.A. administrative and collateral agent. Commitments $425M. Amendment No. 3 (May 2026) raised the letter of credit sublimit from $100M to $125M.
- Maturity: the earlier of May 22, 2028 and 91 days before the final scheduled maturity of any "Material Springer Debt" (defined in the ABL). OPEN: the definition (likely a size threshold; not retrieved).
- Springing fixed charge coverage ratio of 1.0x, tested each quarter in which excess availability is below the greater of $31.875M and 10% of the Line Cap (lesser of commitments and borrowing base).
- Availability (as of Aug 6, 2026, June borrowing base): $388M before letters of credit of about $112M; no borrowings; **net availability about $276M**. Secured debt covenants did not limit ABL capacity at filing date.

**First Lien Term Loan (TLB)** (10-K FY2025)
- Nov 17, 2023, Jefferies Finance agent. Guaranteed by Holdings and certain US, Canadian, English, German and Belgian subsidiaries; secured by substantially all assets of Xerox and those subsidiaries (subject to exceptions). Search results indicate maturity November 2029 and amortization stepping up in 2026 (not verified in the agreement). OPEN: pricing, financial covenants, incremental / ratio-debt capacity.

**Oct 29, 2026 exchange (8-K)**
- Exchange Support Agreement among Xerox, Holdings, guarantors and consenting holders of the 5.500% 2028 notes, existing 1L notes and/or term loans.
- Exchange offer: about $174.0M of 2028 notes (about 31.1% of the outstanding 2028 notes) exchanged, per $1,000, for $690.00 of new 10.250% Senior Secured First Lien Notes due 2030 ("2026 Issue", same terms, priority and collateral as the existing 1L notes) plus common stock (3,872,062 shares of Holdings common stock per the 8-K wording; confirm whether aggregate or per-$1,000 in Ex. 10.1), or in limited cases an extra $60.00 of new 1L notes.
- Early exchange deadline Nov 12, 2026; settlement expected on or about Nov 13, 2026; consenting holders can terminate if early settlement has not occurred by Nov 20, 2026.
- Consenting holders agree (effective at consummation) **not to bring or support any action against the Companies or their agents arising out of or relating to the February 2026 joint venture** and related transactions.
- Amendment No. 2 to the TLB (effective only at settlement) and a supplemental indenture to the April 2025 1L indenture add a **negative covenant limiting the Companies' ability to permit certain entities to incur additional debt or issue additional equity.** Likely aimed at the entities involved in the JV; OPEN: identify those entities in Ex. 10.2.
- Holdings must file a resale registration statement for the new common stock by the settlement date.

Read-through for default timing:
- The **ABL** springing FCCR can only bite if availability falls under about $31.9M (net availability was about $276M at Aug 6, 2026, with no borrowings), so it is not close to a trigger. It is the main place where a conventional ratio test could become a default.
- The **TLB has no maintenance test**, so leverage alone cannot trigger a default there; defaults come from payment failure, other covenant breaches or cross-default.
- The **SSLA minimum revenue test** is the covenant to monitor. Xerox total revenue was $3,768M for the six months and $5,636M for nine months, but the test uses Xerox-branded "Specified Revenue", which is a subset whose size I cannot see in the filings. With total revenue falling 4.7% year over year in Q3 and Lexmark-branded revenue presumably excluded, headroom is unknown. Check the compliance certificate disclosures (Schedule 6 form) and the Q3 10-Q for any disclosure of compliance or waivers. Note the covenant step-up to $5,225M for the 12/31/2026 test.

## 3c. TPG joint venture (IPCo) summary (8-K Feb 17, 2026; Q2 2026 10-Q Notes 1 and 12)

- **Parties:** Xerox Corp and investors including funds managed by Angelo, Gordon & Co. ("TPG"). Closed Feb 17, 2026.
- **Structure:** Xerox Corp contributed the Xerox brand trademarks and related IP ("Contributed IP") to a new entity, XRX Brandco Holdings LLC ("IPCo Holdings"; subsidiary XRX Brandco LLC, "IPCo"), and received Class B Units. IPCo Holdings is a consolidated VIE (thinly capitalized; Xerox is primary beneficiary). At 6/30/26 IPCo Holdings had assets of $908M and liabilities of $458M. Its assets are generally not available to Xerox or Holdings creditors except via distributions allowed under the JV documents.
- **Funding:** investors funded **$405M of senior secured 5-year term loans** (borrower IPCo Holdings, guaranteed by IPCo; Alter Domus (US) LLC agent) and bought **$45M of Class A Units** (mandatory cumulative redemption expected 2031). Together about $450M, shown as debt on Xerox's balance sheet. Costs of $46M at closing (net proceeds about $404M) plus $8M in Q2. Proceeds were distributed to Xerox; uses: liquidity, Lexmark integration, and "opportunistically addressing Holdings' capital structure" (e.g., buying back 2028 notes).
- **Pricing:** term loans at term SOFR (3.00% floor) + 8.125% (or base rate + 7.125%); Class A Units at term SOFR (3.00% floor) + 11.875%. At the floor that is at least about 11.1% on the loans and 14.9% on the units.
- **Amortization:** 4.50% per annum of the closing balance in equal quarterly installments, starting after the quarter ending 9/30/2026; balance due at maturity (fifth anniversary, Feb 2031). Prepayment from certain debt proceeds and excess cash flow.
- **Security and credit support:** IPCo Holdings and IPCo have pledged substantially all assets (the IP, equity, reserve account). SSLA obligations of the Licensees are guaranteed and secured by certain Holdings subsidiaries (the SSLA Guarantee), which includes a quarterly asset coverage ratio (certain guarantor assets vs. term loans and Class A Units, net of IPCo cash), debt/lien/asset-sale limits, and cross-linked defaults.
- **Cash flow supporting the JV debt:** Licensees pay IPCo a **royalty of 2.0% of Specified Revenue** (Xerox-branded revenue), quarterly in arrears, paid within 10 days of quarter-end on an estimate and trued up after the 10-Q/10-K. All cash at IPCo Holdings and IPCo goes into a reserve account usable only for term loan interest and amortization, Class A distributions, permitted overhead.
- **Rough debt service (my estimate, at SOFR floor):** about $45M interest on the loans + $18M amortization + about $7M on Class A, roughly $70M a year, versus a royalty of about $105M a year if Specified Revenue were at the $5,225M covenant level (2.0% x 5,225). Actual royalty depends on actual Xerox-branded revenue and actual SOFR.
- **Term of SSLA:** 10 years with automatic 5-year renewals; IPCo can terminate on a Xerox Event of Default (including payment default, material breach, insolvency, change of control, minimum revenue covenant breach). On termination the licensees lose the right to use the Xerox marks (90-day sell-off period for inventory).
- **Why it matters for other creditors:** the Xerox brand IP was moved outside the credit group that backs the ABL, term loan B and notes (a "drop-down" style financing). The ABL agreement names an "ABL Event of Default" mechanism in the SSLA that lets the ABL collateral agent sell inventory carrying the marks and pay the royalty. The Oct 29, 2026 exchange asks participating holders to release claims relating to this JV and adds a covenant limiting certain entities' ability to incur debt or issue equity.
